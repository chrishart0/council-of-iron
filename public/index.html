INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT

Coordinated arrival

Pass through an ally

Your troops keep their nationality while crossing an ally’s province.

Local recruitment arrow

New local recruits only. Existing garrisons and arriving reinforcements stay here; they do not continue along a chain of arrows.

Rules of engagement

Leave one troop behind. Travel time follows distance. Recall reverses an army at its actual position. Scheduled attacks reserve troops at home until departure. Attack an occupied enemy only after war is declared. Battles take multiple dice rounds; ties favor defenders, and developed provinces add to the highest defender die. Allied attackers combine; reinforcements sent to a defending ally become their troops.

Three commands per ten game seconds. One coordinated attack has one target and counts as one command. Recruitment arrows consume a command only when set or cleared.

DIPLOMACY

Your allegiance

Admission is unanimous. Nobody can be kicked. A new allegiance resets your maturity; joining does not reset existing members.

RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

War and peace

A coalition needs a majority vote to declare war or send and accept peace. Votes and treaty offers expire after 60 game seconds. Peace recalls attacks already under way.
