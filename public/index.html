        Rules of engagement

        Leave one troop behind. Travel time follows distance. Recall reverses an army at its actual position. Scheduled attacks reserve troops at home until departure. Attack an occupied enemy only after war is declared. Battles take multiple dice rounds; ties favor defenders. Reinforcements sent to an ally become their troops.

        Three commands per ten game seconds. One coordinated attack has one target and counts as one command. Recruitment arrows consume a command only when set or cleared.
